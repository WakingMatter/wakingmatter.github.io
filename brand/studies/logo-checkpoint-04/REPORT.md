## Recommendation (not a freeze)

**Working direction: 4 Slender.**

It preserves 04 better than the purely geometric baseline because it keeps 04’s construction (slender parallel monuments, large orb in the doorway) and drops the letter-stem weight that made the same two-stadium idea into an H. The sphere stays large — shrinking it, as an earlier recut did, only makes a thinner H. A whisper of lift (cy 53, not 49) is the one-colour substitute for lost chrome: the ball remains an object in a threshold instead of a crossbar, without Suspended’s planetary jump.

Keep **1 Optical** as the fidelity check (04-literal, centred). Keep **5 Threshold** as the ritual-gate alternate if parallel stadiums still feel extruded; it steps off the board (04’s mono is still parallel). Do not take **2 Suspended** (planet / head at architectural scale) or **3 Held** (tighter counter, more letter).

Honest cost: 16px is fragile. A later small-size master may thicken. Do not thicken the display cut to save the favicon.
